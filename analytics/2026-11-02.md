## Findings
1. The leak is the first 2 seconds on every reel (40-60% gone by 2 s). Everything after decays smoothly — no single bad moment.
2. Reels that open on a full-screen designed graphic (reels 1, 3) read as a slide/ad in the feed; reel 1 (graphic-first, purple card, 31 s) has the worst skip rate.
3. Zero shares/saves/comments: the poll/CTA sits at the end where <10% of viewers still are.
4. Reels are ~2x too long for current retention (avg watch 4-5 s).
5. Hack/utility content wins on YouTube (best like rate); US business news wins less with a 95%-India, 18-34 audience unless it touches them.
6. YouTube picked a mid-reel frame as the tech-stocks thumbnail ("$800B" panel, face small) — set the cover manually.
